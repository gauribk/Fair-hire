What Does the 'Best Match' Label Mean?

No formal data dictionary accompanied this dataset. Rather than assuming what it represents, we analyzed the raw data directly:

  • Target variable: Binary 0/1 column ({{ "%.1f"|format(overall_rate) }}% are marked 1).
  • Skill alignment: Every resume lists 4–7 skills from its job role's designated skill set. There are no mismatched resume/job pairs; the label does not indicate an unqualified applicant.
  • Job descriptions: Exactly 51 unique descriptions exist—one for each role. Custom descriptions do not appear in the training data.
  • Strong gender link: In 32 roles, men are labelled Best Match ≥50 percentage points more often than women; in 17 roles women are marked ≥50 points more often; only 2 roles are roughly neutral.

Conclusion: We treat Best Match as an empirical label. We cannot confirm that it measures actual job suitability or real-world hiring outcomes. Its distribution strongly suggests synthetic or biased labelling.
