Scientific Interpretation & Methodology Honesty
{% if setup == 'standard' %}

Standard models fail to learn real predictive signal: All three algorithms score ~50% accuracy on balanced classes, demonstrating that resume skills and job descriptions do not predict the Best Match label. Beware of naive F1 interpretations: Decision Tree shows artificially high recall because it leans toward labelling nearly everyone as positive. Reporting weak performance candidly is an essential principle of machine learning ethics.

{% else %}

High accuracy in Audit setup is an alert, not an achievement: Model accuracy surges to ~88% solely because the models utilize Gender combined with Job Role. The models have effectively learned to mimic historical bias patterns embedded in the training labels. Deploying such a model in production would institutionalize discrimination.
